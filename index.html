The Belle Behind the Chair

Meet Javel

Every great style starts with a conversation. Before a single strand is touched, I take the time to understand your hair's history, your goals, and the life you actually live — so what we create together looks as good in three weeks as it does the day you leave my chair.

My home salon was built to feel nothing like a salon at all: no rush, no crowd, no noise — just you, your hair, and my full attention. It's where healthy-hair practices meet meticulous technique, and where clients across DC, Maryland and Virginia come to be cared for, not just serviced.

Signature Services

Crafted for Your Hair

Every service begins with a consultation and ends with hair that's healthy, polished, and unmistakably you.

Silk Press

Salon-smooth, glossy straight hair with a healthy bounce — achieved with heat-protective technique that keeps your natural texture intact.

Sew-Ins

Seamless, secure weave installations for natural-looking length and volume, custom-fit and styled to move like your own hair.

Wig Installs

Melted, undetectable wig installs customized to your hairline for a flawless, low-manipulation protective style.

Quick Weaves

A fast, glamorous transformation for clients who want a full new look without the wait — polished, precise, and comfortable.

Knotless Braids

Lightweight, tension-free braids with a natural-looking base — scalp-friendly styling built for comfort and longevity.

Traditional Braids

Classic box braids, cornrows and Fulani styles — clean parts, tight foundations, and designs tailored to your face shape.

Loc Services

Starter locs, retwists, and loc maintenance that supports healthy growth and keeps every loc defined and neat.

Hair Treatments

Deep conditioning, protein and scalp treatments designed to restore strength, moisture, and shine from the roots out.

Natural Hair Styling

Wash-and-goes, twist-outs and defined curl styling that celebrates your natural texture in its healthiest state.

Salon Policies

A Studio Built on Respect

These policies protect the calm, private experience every client deserves.

Appointment Only

This is a private, home-based studio — there are no walk-ins. All services are booked in advance so I can give each client my complete attention.

Deposit Required

A non-refundable deposit secures your appointment and is applied toward your total service cost.

Cancellation Window

Please provide at least 48 hours' notice to reschedule. Cancellations inside this window forfeit the deposit.

Arrival Time

Arrive with hair washed and detangled unless a wash service is booked. Arrivals more than 15 minutes late may need to be rescheduled.

Guests & Children

To preserve the calm of the space, please come alone unless your guest also has an appointment.

Health & Comfort

Please reschedule if you're feeling unwell. The studio is kept clean, private, and comfortable for every guest.
