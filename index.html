Q & A

Good questions.

How do I prepare for my appointment?

Come with your hair washed and blow-dried, with no oils or products added. Bring enough hair for your chosen style; Javel can advise on what to buy when she confirms your booking.

How long does a style take?

Most styles take several hours depending on size, length and styling. Your slot is reserved just for you, with buffer time built in so you’re never rushed.

How much will my style cost?

Prices vary by length, size, styling and add-ons (Boho, Back / Waist / Butt Length). You’ll get your exact quote when Javel confirms your booking, so there are no surprises on the day.

Do I need to pay a deposit?

No deposit needed. Pay at your appointment with Zelle, Cash App or cash.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes. Just reach out at least 2 days before your appointment and Javel will help you find a new time.

Do you do cornrows on short hair?

A minimum of 5 inches of natural hair is needed for cornrows to hold beautifully.

Will I get a reminder?

Yes! You’ll get a reminder text the day before your appointment.
